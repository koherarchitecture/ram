Write 18 short file notings of the kind written in an Indian university or government office, in English, one per line, numbered. Each is one to three sentences, as a section officer, clerk or officer would actually write them in a file, with the usual abbreviations and phrasing (PUC, vide, w.e.f., may kindly, submitted for orders, as per rules, the competent authority, DR, AR, HoD, etc.). Mix them: some name exactly who did or will do each thing (a person by name, a post, or a section), some leave things to nobody in particular (passives, "action may be taken", "the concerned"), and some do both in one noting. Vary the subjects: leave, purchases, repairs, examinations, fees, hostel, transport, recruitment, audit, events. Do not mention food or animals. No commentary, no headings, just the 18 numbered notings.

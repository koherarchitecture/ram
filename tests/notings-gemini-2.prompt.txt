For a writing exercise, write 18 short examples of office file notes in Indian English, one per line, numbered, as staff in a hospital office, a school office, a bank branch and a city municipal office might write them. Each is one to three sentences in the formal style such offices use (for example: "submitted for orders", "may kindly be approved", "vide letter dated", "w.e.f."). In some, say exactly who did or will do each thing (a named person, a post such as the Medical Superintendent or the Branch Manager, or a section); in some, leave it unsaid who does it; in some, do both. Include a few where a file is sent to someone, a few where one officer acts and another approves, and a few with two actions joined by "and". Do not mention food or animals. Only the 18 numbered notes, nothing else.
